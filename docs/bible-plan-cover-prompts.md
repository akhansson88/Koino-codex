# Bible plan cover prompts

All 21 featured covers were generated with the Perchance AI text-to-image generator
(<https://perchance.org/ai-text-to-image-generator>) in **Oil Painting** style,
**Landscape 768×512**, **4 images per prompt**. For each plan the best-looking of the
four candidates was cropped to 16:9 and saved as a 1080×608 quality-90 JPEG.

The series is deliberately language-neutral: the app draws the localized title,
category, day count and reading time on the bottom gradient.
